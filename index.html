01 About me

I am an IT professional specialising in e-governance, and I have been building software since 2012. Today I work as IT Analyst with UNDP's Provincial and Local Governance Strengthening Programme (PLGSP) in Madhesh Province, helping provincial and local governments adopt digital systems that make public services faster and more transparent.

Before UNDP I ran IT for a provincial ministry, led full-stack teams at software companies, and built government portals, CMS platforms and e-learning systems. I also train others — as an LGPAS trainer for local governance and as a Taekwondo instructor — and I keep learning, from cybersecurity to AI.
